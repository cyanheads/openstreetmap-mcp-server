# 0.6.1 — 2026-10-14

## Added

- **`tag_value_match: "list_item"` / `filters[].match: "list_item"`** on `openstreetmap_query_nearby` and `openstreetmap_query_bbox` ([#83](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/83)). Matches one item of a semicolon list, so `cuisine` = `pizza` finds `cuisine=pizza;italian`; `effectiveTag` writes it `cuisine∋pizza`. A list-item match with no value fails `invalid_tag`.
- **Stray-`amenity` hint** ([#84](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/84)). `amenity=museum` and 39 other values OpenStreetMap tags under another key add a `notice` naming it (`tourism=museum`); results are unchanged.
- **`limitNotice`** on the three Overpass tools and `openstreetmap_search_places` ([#105](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/105)) states the applied limit when a larger one was requested.
- **`deferred_ids`** on `openstreetmap_lookup_objects` ([#105](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/105)) returns unique IDs past the first 50 for the next call.
- **`notice` on a non-empty `not_found`** ([#82](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/82)) carries an `openstreetmap_query_raw` existence check.

## Changed

- **`OSM_OVERPASS_MAX_CONCURRENCY` defaults to `1`** ([#102](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/102)), the one query per user the public instances allow. The `rate_limited` recovery says to wait at least 30 s and make fewer calls at once; a 429 keeps its `status` and `Retry-After`.
- **`distance_meters` measures to the nearest point** ([#103](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/103)) on `openstreetmap_query_nearby`: an area containing the point reads 0, a way or relation its nearest vertex or segment. A way-heavy survey past the read ceiling re-runs once ranked by bounding-box center, with a `notice`.
- **Over-cap limits are served up to the cap** ([#105](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/105)) instead of rejected: `limit` above 500 (above 40 on `openstreetmap_search_places`) is clamped, `openstreetmap_lookup_objects` takes up to 500 `osm_ids`, and `max_element_bytes` has no upper bound.
- **Every convenience query declares `[maxsize:268435456]`** ([#106](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/106)); `openstreetmap_query_raw` adds it unless the query declares its own.
- **`not_found` means no Nominatim record** ([#82](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/82)). IDs are trimmed, uppercased, stripped of leading zeros, and deduplicated before the lookup.

## Fixed

- **A 504 or 408 naming a busy dispatcher ends as `overpass_unavailable`** ([#104](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/104)) after one submission; a listed mirror gets the query at once. Other 504s stay `overpass_gateway_timeout`.
- **A response past 64 MiB ends as `result_too_large`** ([#107](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/107)); the body is read through its stream and cancelled at the ceiling, without retry or failover.
- **`ECONNREFUSED` arriving 10 s or more after the request reads `no connection within <N>ms`** ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/100)), as `ConnectionRefused` already did.
