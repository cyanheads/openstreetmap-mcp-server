# 0.7.4 — 2026-11-23

## Changed

- **Request-decided errors log at `notice`.** The 20 `errors[]` entries whose outcome the request alone decides (`no_results`, `no_coverage`, `invalid_*`, `query_error`, `result_too_large`, and the like) declare `severity: 'notice'`, so their `Error in tool:` record leaves `error`; upstream, throttling, and timeout reasons keep it. Responses are unchanged ([#138](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/138)).
- **`openstreetmap-guide` skill** 1.3 → 1.4 for the relation-center and name-hint changes.

## Fixed

- **A relation's `lat`/`lon`** on `openstreetmap_query_nearby` and `openstreetmap_query_bbox` is the midpoint of the bounds Overpass prints, so a relation whose members span 180° of longitude or more (Taveuni, Vanua Levu) is no longer reported 180° away. The queries print relations with `out bb tags;` and nodes and ways with `out center tags;`, and an `openstreetmap_query_nearby` call ranked by center past the 64 MiB read ceiling measures `distance_meters` to the corrected point ([#139](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/139)).
- **Object-valued fields in `content[]`** are escaped one string at a time, so each escaper call is given one string; both surfaces are unchanged ([#141](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/141)).
- **Result cache:** an unranked Overpass result whose response body is over 25 MiB is served uncached, as one over 100,000 elements is. The `offset` descriptions of `openstreetmap_query_raw`, `openstreetmap_query_bbox`, and `openstreetmap_query_nearby` name the ceilings past which paging re-queries ([#141](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/141)).
- **`max_element_bytes`** on `openstreetmap_query_raw` withholds an object-valued `geometry` (an element derived with `convert` or `make`) as it does an array-valued one, listing it in `withheld_keys` with `item_count` as the coordinate positions it holds. `withheldNotice` and the tool descriptions no longer call the withheld keys arrays ([#142](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/142)).
- **Exact-name hint under `within`:** `openstreetmap_query_bbox` prints the boundary's bounds and counts the Overpass index blocks they cover. At most 12 keeps the `openstreetmap_query_raw` route; more prints the `openstreetmap_search_places` arguments alone, with the bounds rounded outward to 4 decimals as `viewbox`; bounds crossing the antimeridian over 12 print no name hint. The raw query had run out its timeout for a state-sized boundary ([#143](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/143)).
- **`language`** on `openstreetmap_search_places`, `openstreetmap_reverse_geocode`, and `openstreetmap_lookup_objects` reads the POSIX and ICU spelling of a script and region: `en_US`, `pt_BR`, `es_419`, `zh_Hant`, `zh_Hant_TW` are forwarded as `en-US`, `pt-BR`, `es`, `zh-Hant`, `zh-Hant-TW`. A locale with an encoding or modifier (`en_US.UTF-8`, `sr_RS@latin`) stays rejected, and `ja_RM` now reads as `ja-RM` ([#144](https://github.com/cyanheads/openstreetmap-mcp-server/issues/144)).
